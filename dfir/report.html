What the new cases test

Each incident has four observation cutoffs, a paired case with decisive evidence withheld, and a benign counterfactual with simulated owner and telemetry verification. The model sees no article title, URL, source label, expected answer or future observation. The same hypothetical action menu is used across all cases, so a named target in a proposal is not proof it is compromised.

Triage uses frozen acceptable sets, including escalation when critical-service judgment is appropriate.

All 108 replays with corroborated or later incident evidence selected an acceptable full containment set. All 36 verified benign controls closed without containment. The 71 action mismatches were confined to host restrictions on early or evidence-withheld cases. These violated this bench’s evidence prerequisite; some organizations may separately permit precautionary access restriction as hardening. The distinction requires human policy adjudication.

Three reordered ActiveMQ snapshots missed the credential-extraction claim. None asserted unsupported encryption, bulk exfiltration or credential extraction. 68 action selections conflicted with an “investigate” triage answer. Raw per-question outputs therefore need cross-answer consistency checks.

Higher thresholds trade one error for another

The frozen yes/no threshold was 0.5. Unsupported host-restriction scores ranged from 0.50 to 0.77 (median 0.64), so these were not high-confidence recommendations. The following is an exploratory analysis of the same responses after seeing results, not a new held-out validation or revised passing score.

Raising the threshold to 0.8 removes the premature selections but misses 76 required action selections. A threshold alone does not solve the workflow. Keep evidence completeness, target verification, critical-service review and cross-answer consistency in deterministic orchestration; calibrate any operating threshold on separate SOC data.

Agentic investigations: completion matters

Jev chose local read-only evidence packs. Twelve incident/control scenarios ran both normally and with injected text: 24 episodes and 52 calls. It was explicitly asked to review all offered sources before the final handoff. 14/24 completed; 10/24 stopped early. Only 2/12 incident episodes completed, compared with 12/12 benign episodes. The completed episodes had correct final triage, action sets and claims (14/14); reporting only that subset would hide the main failure.

Early finishes were the five non-RDP incident families under both variants. Jev stopped after the endpoint pack, leaving identity/lateral and response records unread. Provisional outputs are retained but excluded from final classification accuracy. Tool selection and completeness are tested here; optimal retrieval cost, actual connector behavior and real containment are not.
